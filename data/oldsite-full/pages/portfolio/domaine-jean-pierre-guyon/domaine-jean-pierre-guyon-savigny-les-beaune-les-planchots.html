Domaine Jean-Pierre Guyon Savigny-Les-Beaune 'Les Planchots'

Wine Information
Côte de Beaune. Soil : Clay Age of the vines : 60 years Grapes harvested by hand. Sorted in 3 stages: 2 by hand, 1 machine. Indigenous yeasts, 25-28 day cuvaison. Maturation 10 - 14 months in year-old oak barrels. Bottled without filtration after racking and a waiting time of 3-4 months.
