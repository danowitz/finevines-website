Domaine Brusset Ventoux Les Boudalles

Wine Information
Grapes:  60% Grenache, 15% Carignan, 15% Mourvedre, 10% Clairette.
Soil type: Sandy clay.
Yield:  45 hl / ha.
Harvest:  Manual, variety by variety.
Winemaking:  De-stemming, fermenting 8 days at 26 ° C with daily pumping, two power cuts.
Livestock:  100% in vats for 6 months.
Aging:  3 to 4 years.
Appearance:  Beautiful bright raspberry color.
Aromas: Very aromatic, typified by red fruit (raspberry).
Taste: Supple and harmonious, elegant wine with taste of berries.
