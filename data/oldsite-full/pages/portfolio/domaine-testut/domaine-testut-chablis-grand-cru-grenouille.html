Domaine Testut Chablis Grand Cru Grenouille

Wine Information
Rich and powerful. A strong mineral palate balances the power of the ripe fruit. A wine that will show full potential in 5 to 8 years.

Harvest: Early October.
Age: 50 years. Massale Selection.
Exposure: South.
Soil: Clay and limestone.
Yield: 50 hectoliters per hectare. Manual harvesting.
Breeding: 18 months.
Tasting notes: Long and powerful wine, nice minerality, nice balance.
Vine Age: 60 years
Soil Type: Marls and calcareous clay from the Kimmeridgien
