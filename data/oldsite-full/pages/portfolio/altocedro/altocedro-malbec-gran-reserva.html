Altocedro Malbec Gran Reserva

Wine Information
We strive for a La Consulta-terroir driven Malbec that is elegant with great depth. Very dark, but juicy and driven, with a mouthwatering streak of briar and anise that pushes the muscular core of raspberry, fig and boysenberry fruit. Graphite and black tea notes flash in the background. The finish is long and structured.

91 points ('12), Wine Enthusiast;
100 points and Wine of the Year ('09), Wilfred Wong;
94 points ('08), Wine Advocate; 93 points ('07), Wine Spectator:
