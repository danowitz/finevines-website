Domaine des Comtes Lafon Volnay Premier Cru Santenots-du-Milieu

Wine Information
Size: 3.9 ha (9.64 ac)
Variety: Pinot Noir
Vine Age: Planted in 1944, 1962, 1965, 1978
Terroir: East-facing, shallow red clay soil, hard limestone substrate: a sunny, early-ripening site
Viticulture: Organic (certified), biodynamic
Vinification: 100% de-stemmed, cooled (12-15°C), 4-6 days' whole-grape maceration, 10 days' indigenous yeast fermentation in stainless steel tanks, 1-2 punch-downs per day. 1-2 weeks' settling in tank, gravity transfer to oak barrel (1/3 new). Racking, 18-22 months' aging on fine lees in same barrels, blending in tank.

Volnay 1er Cru Santenots-du-Milieu is about 8 hectares in total surface area, meaning this domaine owns almost half of the appellation. Their parcel has a high proportion of old vines. When replanting they use a combined method, with a blend of massal selection and clonal selection (at least 5 different types). Pruning is mostly Guyot, with some Cordon Royat.
