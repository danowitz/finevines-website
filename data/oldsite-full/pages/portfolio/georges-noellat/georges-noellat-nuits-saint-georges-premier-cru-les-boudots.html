Georges Noëllat Nuits-Saint-Georges Premier Cru Les Boudots

Wine Information
Size: 1.20 ha (2.96 ac)
Variety: Pinot Noir
Vine Age: 90 years old on average
Terroir: On border with Vosne, steep (18%) slope, well-draining stony soil
Viticulture: Lutte raisonnée, treatments carried out only in reaction to a real, imminent threat
Vinification: At least 70% de-stemmed, 4-8 days cool maceration, 2-3 weeks indigenous yeast fermentation and maceration (punch-downs rare). Aged for 14 to 20 months in barrel (50%-75% new oak).

This 1er Cru lies at the very north of Nuits-St-Georges, bordering Vosne-Romanée 1er Cru Aux Malconsorts.
