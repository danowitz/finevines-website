Château Puy-Servain Calabre Terrement

Wine Information
Tasting: lemon yellow color, fresh pineapple quince aromas, superb sweetness on the attack, which leaves a fresh finish with good acidity.

Place: Puy Servain whose origin comes from "Puy" = summit and from "Servain" = strong wind, because it is a very windy climax.
Type of soil: clay-limestone; very overhauled. Heterogeneous soil with very calcareous areas at the top of the hills
Serving suggestion: drink very cold, as an aperitif, with foie gras, white meats and parsley cheeses.
Grape varieties: 100% semillon
