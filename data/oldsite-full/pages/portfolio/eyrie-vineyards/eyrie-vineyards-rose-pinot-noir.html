Eyrie Vineyards Rosé of Pinot Noir

Wine Information
Not just another fruit bomb, the aromatics of this wine create an aromatic spectrum ranging from bright strawberries and pink grapefruit, to fresh turned loam and savory herbs. The palate is long and precise with a round, silky texture.  The evolving finish compliments and echoes the primary fruit of the palate.

Viticulture & Vinification
The grapes came from the old organic vines of the Cattrall Brothers vineyard. As the pick actually came in over different days throughout harvest, the tart-sweet pink juice fermented in a wide variety of fermenters, from neutral barrels to our big open wooden tank. The range of fermentations provided a few extra facets.
