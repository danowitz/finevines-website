Alpha Omega

Alpha Omega, a family owned boutique winery, is located in the heart of Napa Valley on the Rutherford Bench.  Alpha Omega creates a wine that uniquely expresses the essence of the terroir of this famous wine region.  Talented winemaker, Jean Hoefliger, has teamed up with one of the world’s finest palates, Michel Rolland, to create wines that express their passion for excellence as well as the unique terroir that is Napa Valley.  Their vineyards are located in Rutherford, St. Helena, Howell Mntn. And Spring Valley.
