Scotto Cellars

Today Anthony Dominick III, his brothers and sisters represent the 5th Generation of Scotto family vintners.  Producing 40 brands, Anthony and Dominick being one, the wines are sourced from the Russian River Valley and Napa Valley. 

Anthony Scotto, Sr., of strong Italian heritage and tradition, moved from Brooklyn, NY to California in 1963 but his wine making career started in the 1940.  The Villa Armando label is one of the oldest US wine brands.  Today, Anthony III and his siblings represent the 5th generation of family winemakers at Scotto Cellars.  The winery produces quality wines with over 30 different brands worldwide.
