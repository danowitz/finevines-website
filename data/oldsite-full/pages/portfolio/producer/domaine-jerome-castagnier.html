Domaine Jerome Castagnier

Location: Morey Saint Denis, Côte de Nuits, Burgundy
Size: 4 ha (9.88 ac)
Varieties: Pinot Noir, Chardonnay, Aligoté
Viticulture: Lutte intégrée with biodynamic methods
Vinification: Whites directly pressed, reds mostly de-stemmed, indigenous yeast fermentation. Aged in barrel (0-50% new oak depending on appellation and vintage).
