Domaine Des Corbillières

Dominique Barbou is a meticulous winegrower, who farms 26 hectares of vines in Oisly, 8 of them red. His great grandfather, Fabel Barbou, was the first to bring Sauvignon to the region: he'd planted a vine against his house, apparently, and noticed how well it grew.

Barbou works with natural yeasts, and although he's not organic, he doesn't use any chemical fertilizers, just organic material.
