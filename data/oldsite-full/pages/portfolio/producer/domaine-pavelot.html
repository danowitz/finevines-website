Domaine Pavelot

For several generations, the Domaine Jean-Marc and Hugues Pavelot located in Savigny-les-Beaune (Côte de Beaune, Burgundy) has been passed down from father to son, thus guaranteeing the know-how that makes its reputation.

Today, the property covers 13 hectares, mainly in the town of Savigny-les-Beaune but also in that of Beaune and Aloxe-Corton.

Our production will allow you to discover a dozen wines of very varied typicity. These differences are closely linked to the concept of terroir, a particularity of Burgundy viticulture. Each appellation or climate is therefore the expression of a fairly complex association between the geology of the place, its topography and even its history.

This diversity is very present in the Savigny-les-Beaune vineyard which covers two hills and the valley that connects them. Its altitude is between 220 and 360 meters. The nature of the soil is very varied and its exposure is oriented south, southeast and northeast.

Pinot Noir for the reds and Chardonnay for the whites are the exclusive grape varieties of our great Burgundies.

Location: Savigny-Lès-Beaune, Côte de Beaune, Burgundy
Size: 13 ha (32.12 ac)
Varieties: Pinot Noir (90%), Chardonnay (10%)
Viticulture: Lutte raisonnée
Vinification: 100% de-stemmed (except 10% whole cluster for La Dominode), indigenous yeasts, minimal new oak, unfined, lightly filtered if necessary
