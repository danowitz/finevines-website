Château de la Bouyère

The Queyrens family has owned Chateau de la Bouyere for more than a century. They preserved the knowledge acquired by the generations before them. This vineyard is located at the top of Donzac, 45 km in the south of Bordeaux. Grape production, winemaking and aging all take place on the family estate.
