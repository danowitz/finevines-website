Domaine Paul Autard

In this inter-generational exchange,knowledge of the Châteauneuf-du-Pape terroir, the vegetative cycle and the work of planting vines is one of the essential keys.

As in all major appellations, the history of the Autard family is first and foremost a story of winegrowers who pass on from generation to generation a precious sum of knowledge, observations and work in the vineyard.

The Autard family has, over the years, worked on an old farm in Courthézon and then in 2005 built a new cellar in order to optimize the work of wine making.

Jean-Paul Autard inherited this teaching and this knowledge. He brings his own ideas, experiments and intuitions to make Domaine Paul Autard one of the big names in this prestigious appellation.
