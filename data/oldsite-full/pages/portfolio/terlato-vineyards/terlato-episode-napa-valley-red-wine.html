Terlato Episode Napa Valley Red Wine

Wine Information
Made with a blend of classic varietals, including Cabernet Sauvignon, Merlot and a touch of Petit Verdot, this wine is deep ruby in color with a brooding fruit aroma accented by layers of anise and dark chocolate. The rich mouthfeel has a velvety structure and offers an incredible lingering finish.

Style: Complex, Full-bodied, Rich, Structured

Flavor: Anise, Chocolate, Dark Fruit

Grapes: Cabernet Sauvignon, Merlot, Cabernet Franc, Petit Verdot

Fermentation: Fermented in stainless steel tanks, with daily pumpovers. 

Aging: 21 months cave aged in 100% new French oak barrels, 14 months aging in bottle prior to release.
