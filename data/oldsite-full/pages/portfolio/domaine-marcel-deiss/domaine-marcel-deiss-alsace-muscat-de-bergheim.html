Domaine Marcel Deiss Alsace Muscat de Bergheim

Wine Information
This wine is produced on the best terroirs of Bergheim (Altenberg and Pflänzer). The limestone soils are fertile with good water reserves. On this type of terroir, Muscat should be monitored carefully for its yield and acidity levels. However, the balance is ensured by bunch-thinning and by early harvesting, producing a very interesting, terroir-focused Muscat.

Variety: Ottonel Muscat, White Alsatian Muscat, Pink Alsatian Muscat
Vine Age: Planted 1977-1997
Vine Density: 7,000 per ha
Terroir: Altenberg and Pflänzer, clay-limestone with fossils
Viticulture: Certified organic (Ecocert) and biodynamic
Vinification: Whole-cluster pressing, indigenous yeast fermentation. Aged for 12 months on fine lees.
Sweetness level: Dry/ fruity
