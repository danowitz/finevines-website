Chateau de Laubade Armagnac VSOP

Wine Information
Château de Laubade V.S.O.P. is a blend of several different eaux-de-vie, the youngest being 6 years of age and in which the grape varieties of Ugni-Blanc and Folle Blanche are highly present.

Tasting Notes
The amber colour is of a beautiful brightness.
The nose is fruity, in particular with vanilla and prune notes. It also develops aromas of white flowers.
It is soft in the mouth, well-balanced and the aromatic persistence lasts longer than five minutes.
