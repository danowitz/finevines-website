Domaine Paul Jaboulet Aîné Hermitage Maison Bleue Rouge

 Wine Information
Replacing La Petite Chapelle in the Jaboulet lineup is the Hermitage La Maison Bleue. Rather than being a selection of lots that didnt make the cut for La Chapelle, its more terroir-based, with the wine coming from the eastern (non-granitic) side of the appellation. Because of that, its softer in style, with a rich, velvety texture. Cedar and spice notes frame cassis and cola notes, lingering through the long, supple finish.
