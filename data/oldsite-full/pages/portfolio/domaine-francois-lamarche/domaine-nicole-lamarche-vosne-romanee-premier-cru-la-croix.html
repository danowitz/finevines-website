Domaine Nicole Lamarche Vosne-Romanée Premier Cru La Croix Rameau

Wine Information
Vinification: Hand picked, 80 to 100% de-stemmed depending on the vintage. Vatting period of about 15 to 18 days, in open oak vats at a low temperature (controlled by thermo-regulation).

Maturation: In oak casks for between 16 and 20 months (between 60 and 100% new oak depending on the vintage). Bottled with no fining and no filtering.

Aging in Bottles: At least four years.

Drink It...: With all kinds of red meat, game and cheeses.

Tasting Notes: Display tasting notes for this wine.

Soil: Clay-limestone

Orientation: Due east

Surface Area: 0.52 acres

Grape Variety: 100% Pinot Noir

Average Age of the Vines: 40 year old

Average Yield: 42 hectolitres per hectare maximum: about 900 bottles
