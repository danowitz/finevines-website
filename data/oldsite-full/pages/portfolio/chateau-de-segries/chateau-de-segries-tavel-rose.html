Château de Ségriès Tavel Rosé

Wine Information
Beautiful ripe cherry and red berry aromas with floral scents. Good concentration on the palate, flavorful and perfectly balanced. Good acidity and mineral backbone make it a great match with Provencal or Asian cuisine, grilled meat, fresh fruit salads.

Appellation: Tavel AOC
Wine Type: Rosé
Varietal(s): 50% Grenache, 30% Cinsault, 10% Clairette, 10% Syrah
Age of vines: 60 years
Soil type: Pure silica, white lauzes, sand, clay, & pebble stones
Vinification: 'Vin de saignee'. 100% destemmed; overnight maceration in cement tank; low temperature fermentation.
Pairings: Provencal and Asian cuisine, Spicy dishes, Meats, Barbecues, Strawberry Salad
