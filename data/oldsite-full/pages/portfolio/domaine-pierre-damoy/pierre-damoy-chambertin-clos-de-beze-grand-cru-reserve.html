Pierre Damoy Chambertin ​​​​​​​Clos de Bèze Grand Cru Réserve

Wine Information
Clos de Bèze Réserve, formerly Vielles Vignes : since 2002 a selection from old vines planted in 1920 in the far southern section of the Clos Only made in vintages when Pierre notices a definite superior level of quality . Always strictly limited availability.The wine is of monolithic proportions in the mouth beating all comers by its density , richness and length. 300→ 900 bottles maximum,

"A background touch of wood serves as a backdrop to the exuberantly spicy dark berry fruit, earth and soft floral nuances. There is good concentration to the powerful and mineral-driven broad-shouldered flavors that possess an abundance of palate coating dry extract that also buffers the very firm tannic spine on the balanced and strikingly persistent finish. This should be great in time but patience will definitely be required." 92-95/100 - Burghound

`Pierre Damoy has one of the largest holdings in Clos de Bèze, with 5 hectares. His prices are almost reasonable by the standards of the Grand Cru, and quality is high. Stylish, sexy and very smooth, this has that winning combination of sweet blackberry and black cherry fruit, excellent structure, appealing oak and a long, creamy finish. Drink 2018-30'
