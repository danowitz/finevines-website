Domaine Lécheneaut Vosne-Romanée

Wine Information
Total Surface : 10 ouvrées (1 ouvrée = 4 ares 28)
Grape Variety : Pinot Noir
Average Age of the Vine : 57 years old
Nature of the Soil : Lieu-dit : Aux Ravioles
The vineyard is located at the bottom of the hill on lightly chalky soils mixed with marl and clay, according to the depth that can go over one meter.
Harvesting : Manual only
Average Production : 2400 Bottles
Wine Characteristics : This is a vermeil wine, with a ruby shade that sometimes leans towards garnet and dark purple. Very ripe fruit with a spicy background creates an assorted bouquet of strawberry, raspberry, blueberry, and blackcurrant. The complexity of refined aromas evolves with age to cherry spirits, leather, and fur. A wine to be aged, of course, balancing richness and tannins. It must develop its structure and boldness in the bottle.
