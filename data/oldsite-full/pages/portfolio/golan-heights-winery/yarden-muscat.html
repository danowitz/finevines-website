Yarden Muscat

The Wine

The very aromatic Yarden Muscat features orange blossom, marmalade and lemon zest notes layered with nutmeg and vanilla bean. The wine is unabashedly sweet, yet retains an elegant drinkability. Yarden Muscat is ready to be enjoyed yet ages beautifully, taking on a richer marmalade character.
