## What to ask for

High-resolution bottle photography, one image per wine, on a plain or transparent background — the shape their own marketing materials already use. No watermark. A shared folder or a zip is fine; we handle resizing and cropping.

Worth saying plainly to each importer: their wines are the ones currently showing a grey placeholder on a live catalog their customers browse. That is usually enough.
